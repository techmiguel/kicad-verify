# Coverage: what each verifier catches and what it does not

Generated by `kicadverify checks --markdown` from `kicadverify/data/checks.yaml`; do not edit by hand.

A requirement is VERIFIED only when every verifier behind it ran, found nothing outside its acceptance criterion and covered its whole scope. Even then it means "this kind of defect was not detected within this coverage", never "the board is correct". The limits below are printed next to each requirement in every report. Measured detection rates: [RESULTS.md](RESULTS.md).

## KiCad checks

| Verifier | Modes | Inputs | Covers | Does not cover |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PCB-ERC-001 | full | .kicad_sch | Unconnected pins, pin-type conflicts, missing power flags | Wrong symbol pinout vs the datasheet; component values; function |
| PCB-DRC-001 | full | .kicad_pcb, .kicad_pro, .kicad_dru | Clearances, drill sizes, overlaps and silkscreen, against the rules stored in the board (library drift is PCB-HYGIENE-001) | Whether those rules are the right ones for the fab or the application (FAB-RULES-001 checks the fab side; mains isolation is a project requirement); signal integrity; thermal |
| PCB-HYGIENE-001 | full | .kicad_sch, .kicad_pcb, symbol and footprint library tables | Symbols and footprints missing from the installed libraries or differing from them, wire ends off the connection grid; reported, never gating | Depends on the libraries installed where the check runs (a CI without the project's libraries reports every part); the design is checked on its embedded copies by PCB-ERC-001 and PCB-DRC-001 |
| PCB-CONN-001 | full | .kicad_pcb | Connections in the board ratsnest that are not routed | Whether the netlist is the intended one |
| PCB-PARITY-001 | fast, full | .kicad_sch, .kicad_pcb | Same references, values, footprints and net per pin in schematic and PCB (values compared as numbers when both parse, so 4k7 = 4.7k = 4700 and 100n = 100nF; a net name KiCad derives from a pin, Net-(...) or unconnected-(...), is compared by the pins on it) | Errors present in both. Fields other than the value (MPN, voltage or tolerance fields). PCB-only parts (MountingHole, Fiducial, TestPoint, board_only) are ignored |
| PCB-PINMAP-001 | fast, full | .kicad_sch, .kicad_pcb | Symbol pins without a pad (unless another pin of the same symbol on the same net has a pad carrying it, as when one contact is drawn as two pins); electrical pads without a symbol pin (wrong package or numbering) | A pin number that exists in both but means something else (MOD-PINOUT-001) |

## Board checks (direct `.kicad_pcb` parse)

| Verifier | Modes | Inputs | Covers | Does not cover |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| PCB-FOOT-001 | fast, full | .kicad_pcb, verification/pcb/approved_footprints.txt | Footprints not in the approved list | Whether the approved list itself is right; approve each entry against the datasheet |
| PCB-MODEL-001 | fast, full | .kicad_pcb | Footprints that place a body on the board (they have pads and are not logos, markings, test points, mounting holes, fiducials, net ties, solder jumpers or wire pads, Tag-Connect cable footprints) without a 3D model | A model of the wrong part or wrongly oriented |
| PCB-PADNET-001 | fast, full | .kicad_pcb | Electrical pads without a net (plated mounting-hole pads excluded; a pad that is no pin of its symbol is reported once, by PCB-PINMAP-001) | Whether the pin was meant to be NC (each one must be waived with the reason) |
| PCB-WIDTH-001 | fast, full | .kicad_pcb | On nets whose name looks like power (not a rail's control or status signals such as `VBUS_EN`, `VIN_SENSE`), chains of tracks narrower than the limit, leaving out stubs that end at a single capacitor or test point. The limit is the IPC-2221 width for the net's declared current (params.net_currents) with the board's stackup copper thickness and width_temp_rise_c, else the project's min_power_width_mm; with neither, a net with tracks under power_review_width_mm is a coverage gap asking for its current. Findings name the pads the thin tracks reach | Which branch of a net carries the declared current (every non-stub thin chain is judged against the whole net current); planes and zones; via current; power nets with unusual names (power_net_patterns); IPC-2221 is conservative for short tracks |
| PCB-PINS-001 | fast, full | .kicad_pcb, verification/pcb/pins.yaml | Pins listed in pins.yaml compared with the PCB nets | Pins not listed; transcription errors in pins.yaml |
| PCB-HOLE-001 | fast, full | .kicad_pcb, params.mounting_holes | Mounting-hole position and drill vs the expected values | Whether the expected values match the enclosure (the board interface lets a mechanical tool check that) |
| PCB-KEEPOUT-001 | fast, full | .kicad_pcb | Outer-layer tracks, vias and pads of other nets inside the washer radius | Copper zones; screw heads larger than the configured radius |
| ISO-SEP-001 | fast, full | .kicad_pcb, .kicad_pro (net classes), params.isolation.barriers | Smallest copper-to-copper distance between the two sides of each declared barrier, per copper layer (tracks, vias, pads, zone fills). A straight-line distance at or above the requirement implies the PCB creepage and clearance meet it | Component bodies and leads above the board, relay/transformer internals, distance through the insulation between layers, creepage around slots (a shortfall on a board with cutouts is an unchecked item, not a FAIL), pollution degree and material group (they are in the required value the project declares). kicad-verify ships no normative distances |

## Circuit checks (schematic netlist)

| Verifier | Modes | Inputs | Covers | Does not cover |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| CIR-POL-001 | fast, full | .kicad_sch | LEDs whose anode resolves to GND, reverse-biased LEDs, diodes forward-shorting a rail, reversed flyback diodes across relays/inductors, forward-biased clamps, reversed polarized capacitors | Parts on nets of unknown voltage (listed as unchecked in the coverage); footprint pin 1 vs symbol (MOD-FOOT-001, HUM-POL-001) |
| CIR-REG-001 | fast, full | .kicad_sch | Fixed regulators whose output voltage (from the part value) differs from the rail; input below output + dropout | Adjustable regulators and part values the patterns do not recognise; thermal dissipation |
| CIR-LED-001 | fast, full | .kicad_sch | LED current outside led_min_ma..led_max_ma, from rail/logic level, series resistor and a colour-based Vf | LEDs driven by constant-current drivers or through more than one resistor (listed as unchecked) |
| CIR-BJT-001 | fast, full | .kicad_sch | NPN base driven without a resistor, base current above gpio_max_ma | PNP and MOSFET drive; saturation margin vs collector load |
| CIR-FLOAT-001 | fast, full | .kicad_sch | Enable/reset/boot pins with no other connection | Pins tied to the wrong level (MOD-STRAP-001) |
| KH-* | fast, full | .kicad_sch, .kicad_pcb, Gerbers | 60+ detectors - capacitor derating, regulator feedback, decoupling, bus pull-ups, protection mapping, edge distance, DFM, test points | See kicad-happy's own documentation. error gates as FAIL, warning as WARN, info goes to the reviewer only |

## Fabrication and manufacturability

| Verifier | Modes | Inputs | Covers | Does not cover |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| FAB-RULES-001 | fast, full | .kicad_pro, .kicad_dru, fab profile | Board minimum clearance, net-class clearances and custom-rule clearances at least as strict as the fab's minimum spacing, so a clean DRC implies manufacturable spacing | Rule conditions are not evaluated (a custom rule is compared by its value only); the fab profile values themselves (check its source) |
| FAB-DFM-001 | fast, full | .kicad_pcb, fab profile | Measured track width, via drill/pad/annular ring, plated-hole drill and annular ring (via limits for thermal vias inside an SMD pad), hole-to-hole distance (slots as capsules), copper-to-board-edge distance (tracks, vias, pads, filled zones), board size, layer count and thickness; a length is outside a limit only by more than 0.1 mil (2.54 µm), the resolution of a limit stated in mil; identical findings are grouped | Copper-to-copper spacing (FAB-RULES-001 + DRC); arcs approximated by 16 segments; non-rectangular custom pads approximated by their bounding box; silkscreen and solder mask |
| FAB-GERBER-001 | full | Gerbers | Missing copper, mask or outline layers (X2 FileFunction), in every export set (each directory of Gerbers and each zip archive holding them) | Paste and silkscreen (optional); Gerbers without X2 attributes |
| FAB-STALE-001 | full | Gerbers, .kicad_pcb | For each export set (a directory of Gerbers, or a zip archive such as the one uploaded to the fab, compared separately), re-plots the exported layers with kicad-cli (explicit layers and Gerber format, not the board's last plot settings; drill/place origin when the board plots with it) and compares, within 6 µm whatever the exported precision or unit, the positions of apertures with a function (pads, tracks, vias, outline) and of every mask and paste opening | Legend (silkscreen) layers; aperture sizes (a pad resized in place, a mask expansion change); mask openings over non-plated holes (KiCad versions plot them differently); drill marks and other function-less apertures |
| FAB-DRILL-001 | fast, full | .drl, .kicad_pcb | Drill diameters, counts and positions (within 0.02 mm, absolute or aux origin) vs the PCB, for each export set (directory or zip archive) separately | Slots; plated/non-plated mix-ups; Excellon without a decimal point (diameters only, positions listed as unchecked) |
| FAB-BOM-001 | fast, full | BOM .csv, .kicad_pcb | Assembled parts missing from the BOM or extra in it (grouped designators such as B1-B4 expanded; fitted padless parts such as heatsinks accepted; lines marked DNP must be DNP on the PCB), footprint per reference, lines with no supplier or manufacturer part number in any part-number column. With several BOM files and none chosen in params.fab.bom, the one the board agrees with best is checked and the others are named | Whether the supplier code is the right part (value, voltage, tolerance); stock |
| FAB-CPL-001 | fast, full | CPL .csv, .kicad_pcb | Missing parts, position not at the pad centre (after detecting origin and Y direction), wrong side; CSV or KiCad's ASCII position file (inches or mm); through-hole parts left out of an SMD-only file are warnings. With several placement files and none chosen in params.fab.cpl, the one the board agrees with best is checked and the others are named | Rotations - every fab house applies its own offsets (HUM-CPL-001) |

## Project assertions

| Verifier | Modes | Inputs | Covers | Does not cover |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ASSERT | fast, full | .kicad_pcb, .kicad_sch | The property stated in the requirement's `check:` block (pin_net, value, footprint, field, net_exists, board_size, layer_count, track_width) | Only what the assertion states |
| GEN-INTENT-001 | fast, full | verification/pcb/design_intent.md | Fields of design_intent.md (lines like `- Field: value`) that are filled; each empty field is an unchecked item. The intent digest is recorded in every report, review and attestation | Whether what is declared is true or complete beyond the listed fields |

## Independent reviewer

| Verifier | Modes | Inputs | Covers | Does not cover |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| REVIEW | review | review bundle, datasheets | Judgement requirements where a rule cannot decide; every quote is searched verbatim in the cited file | Only what is in the bundle and project files (no web); non-deterministic; can share blind spots with the tools that generated the design |

## Statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| VERIFIED | Every verifier ran, found no defect beyond the acceptance criterion and checked its whole scope (unchecked items waived one by one by a person) |
| FAILED | A verifier found a defect that is not waived |
| NOT_VERIFIABLE | A verifier ran but could not decide: missing input, partial coverage, unconfirmed reviewer claim |
| NOT_RUN | No verifier produced a result for the current design (fast mode, review not run, no valid sign-off) |

## General limits

- kicad-cli reads the saved file, not what is on screen in KiCad.
- `kicad-cli pcb drc --schematic-parity` hangs on KiCad 10.0.5 with at least one real project; parity is computed by kicad-verify instead.
- The checks and the reviewer can share blind spots with the tools that generated the design; the human sign-offs exist for that reason.
